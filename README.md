# My Favorite Movie
## Miracle in Cell No. 7 (2019)

So this is my favorite movie **Miracle in Cell No.7** by **Mr. Aga Muhlach** a Filipino drama/comedy adaptation released on 2019. A story about a father and daughter that show a fathers love and injustice in social class., and a sacrifice. I know this is a **heartbreaking** movie because it shows an innocent person being imprisoned without people knowing the *full side of the story*. It shows how those in power use their high position and connections to their advantage, and while *injustice* is denied and ==abuse of power prevails==.
